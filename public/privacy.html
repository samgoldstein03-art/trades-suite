Privacy Policy

Draft. Owner review required.

This is an owner-completion checklist, not a final Privacy Policy. The owner has not supplied or approved a complete account of how a real installation handles personal information.

Content still needed from the owner

Confirm the actual installation, providers, and operating practices before replacing this draft with a reviewed policy. No retention period, storage location, or privacy promise has been invented here.

Who is responsible for personal information?
Supply the responsible business or provider's legal identity and the real contact for privacy questions and requests.
What information is collected, and why?
Confirm which business details, customer contact details, notes, message records, quote information, job addresses, appointments, progress updates, and response activity are collected in the actual installation and how each is used.
Which providers receive information?
Identify the hosting, text-message, email, and other providers actually configured, what they receive, and the locations or transfers the owner needs to disclose. Do not list an unconfigured service as active.
How long is information kept?
Supply the actual retention, backup, export, and deletion practices. This draft sets no retention or deletion deadline.
How can people make a choice or request help?
Confirm the process and real contact for privacy requests, corrections, and communication preferences. Describe the implemented text and quote-email opt-out controls in the reviewed policy.
What happens in the browser?
Confirm any browser storage, cookies, analytics, and tracking used by the installation. Review the browser-only sample workspace separately from a hosted customer database.
What safeguards and review apply?
Describe verified access and security practices without adding unverified claims. Review private job-link sharing, replacement and revocation, including who may see an address or published update. Record the owner's approval and effective date after review with an appropriate adviser.
